Support

Tell us what happened, not who the lead was.

Email ivorjpc@gmail.com for BoothRelay support. The app is still in release preparation, so App Store download and billing support are not yet active.

Include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The BoothRelay version and build number shown in the app or Settings.
  • The screen and action that failed, plus what you expected.
  • Whether the problem concerns manual capture, Contacts, export, deletion, or a purchase state.

Do not include

Do not send real lead names, contact details, conversation notes, badge images, exported files, Apple Account credentials, payment details, or other private event data. A redacted screenshot is useful only when it contains no private data.

Data controls

Use BoothRelay's Privacy & Data screen to create a local JSON copy or delete all app-owned data. Deleting the app also removes its local container, subject to iOS behavior and any device backup you control. Files you previously shared to another app must be deleted from that destination separately.

Purchases

When subscriptions become available, use Restore Purchases in BoothRelay for an existing Apple purchase. Use Apple's purchase history and subscription settings for billing, cancellation, or refund requests.
