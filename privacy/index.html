Privacy notice

Your event data stays under your control.

The current BoothRelay app has no developer-operated account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, tracking SDK, or general network client. Its privacy manifest declares tracking off and no collected-data categories.

Data you enter

BoothRelay can store events, names, company and role details, email addresses, phone numbers, consent records, conversation notes, priorities, next actions, qualification rules, export mappings and export history. It also stores app setup and preferences. This data is kept in the app's local container on your device.

Permissions

The app asks for Camera, Microphone, or Contacts access only when you choose a feature that needs it. The manual capture path works without badge scanning, speech capture, or Contacts access. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

When data leaves the app

  • Export and share actions send only the content you choose to the destination you select.
  • Saving to Contacts gives the selected contact fields to Apple's Contacts service.
  • If subscriptions are offered, Apple processes the purchase, payment, and subscription status. BoothRelay does not receive your payment-card details.

BoothRelay does not claim that exported data remains local after you share it. The receiving app or service applies its own privacy terms.

Export, retention, and deletion

You can export a local JSON copy and create basic CSV or vCard exports. Existing records, basic exports, export history, and generated files remain accessible if a Pro entitlement expires.

You can delete individual leads and events. Delete All requires explicit confirmation and clears BoothRelay's events, leads and follow-ups, receipts, rules, presets, duplicate decisions, setup state, and app-generated export directory. It does not delete source files or copies already shared outside the app container.

Contact

Questions can be sent to ivorjpc@gmail.com. Please do not include lead names, notes, badge images, phone numbers, email addresses, or exported records in an initial support message.

Changes

This notice will be updated if BoothRelay adds a backend, analytics, a third-party SDK, or another practice that changes how data is handled.
